Google 连接器

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日。Doo 是测试中的 iOS AI 助手，Google 连接器仍在测试，尚未通过公众访问审核。

如何连接与使用

  1. 打开 Doo 的连接器列表，选择 Gmail、Google Calendar 或 Google Drive。阅读连接页的数据处理说明，再点击“连接”。仅打开详情不会授权。
  2. 在 Google 页面核对 Doo、当前账号及本次服务请求的权限。你可以拒绝或取消。Google 权限只适用于你同意的范围。
  3. 回到 Doo 后，在聊天中选择对应连接器并提出具体要求。助手会使用相关工具处理任务；连接本身不会开始全量同步。

相关内容由 Cloudflare 上的 Doo 后端处理，需要 AI 时发送给 OpenAI；任务需要文件处理或云电脑时可能进入 E2B。任务上下文、回复、恢复记录和保存文件可能包含这些内容。详见Google 数据政策说明。

Gmail

可以搜索并阅读邮件、会话、草稿和附件，创建或更新供你审阅的草稿。例如：“总结本周关于旅行的邮件，并准备一封回复草稿。”当前不能发送邮件、删除邮件或更改标签。Google 的 gmail.compose 权限自身包含发送能力，Doo 当前工具没有暴露发送操作。

请求 openid、userinfo.email、gmail.readonly、gmail.compose；不申请整个邮箱的永久删除权限。

Google Calendar

可以列出日历、查询事件与空闲时间，按你要求创建、修改或删除具体事件。例如：“查一下明天下午有没有空，帮我安排半小时准备会议。”修改和删除校验事件版本；是否给参与者发通知取决于操作参数与授权。

请求 openid、userinfo.email、calendar.calendarlist.readonly、calendar.events、calendar.events.freebusy；不申请修改日历共享权限。

Google Drive

可以查找已有文件、读取支持的内容或导出 Google 文档，创建文本文件、文件夹及权限允许的文件副本。例如：“找出项目计划，帮我整理成一个文本文件。”当前不能覆盖原文件、任意上传二进制文件，或结构化编辑 Docs／Sheets／Slides。单个返回文件最多 8 MiB。

请求 openid、userinfo.email、drive.readonly、drive.file。跨既有文件搜索需要只读访问；写入限于 Google 对 drive.file 授予的文件范围。

断开、撤权和删除数据

  1. 停止 Doo 使用：打开连接器详情，点“解除连接”并确认。配置和加密授权资料会保留以便重连；已有聊天、任务记录及文件不会被删除。
  2. 在 Google 撤权：前往 Google 账号 → 第三方连接，找到 Doo 并撤销相应访问。同一 Doo 应用的多个 Google 服务可能一起需要重新授权。
  3. 管理保存内容：在 Doo 中删除相应聊天或保存文件，可移除这些可见内容，但不能把单条内容删除当作已经清除了全部任务恢复副本。需要完整云端内容和凭据清理时，使用账号注销或联系隐私邮箱。
  4. 注销 Doo：在设置的账号页选择注销账号，完成新的 Apple 或邮箱身份验证。请求接受后账号停止访问，后台继续清理云端内容、任务与凭据。失败时清理可能仍在进行，以 App 中的注销回执为准。交易与必要删除回执的保留见隐私政策。
  5. 外部副本：在 Google 里创建的草稿、事件和文件不会随 Doo 注销自动删除。你导出的文件及其他设备的独立副本也需另行管理。

Doo Google connectors

Updated October 20, 2026. Doo is an iOS AI assistant in testing. Google connectors remain in testing and are not approved for public Google access.

Connect and use

  1. Open the connectors list and select Gmail, Google Calendar or Google Drive. Read the data disclosure before tapping Connect. Opening details alone does not authorize access.
  2. Review Doo, your account and the requested service scopes on Google's consent page. You may decline or cancel.
  3. Return to Doo, select the connector in chat and request a specific task. Connecting does not begin whole-account synchronization.

Relevant content is processed by Doo's Cloudflare backend and OpenAI for AI assistance, with E2B used when a requested file-processing or cloud-computer task needs it. Context, replies, recovery records and saved files may contain this content. Read the Google data disclosure.

Supported features and scopes

  • Gmail: search and read messages, threads, attachments and drafts; create or update reviewable drafts. No sending, message deletion or label modification tool. Google's gmail.compose scope itself includes sending capability. Scopes: openid, userinfo.email, gmail.readonly, gmail.compose.
  • Calendar: list calendars, read events, check availability, create, update or delete requested events. Updates and deletion check the event version. Guest notifications depend on the operation. Scopes: openid, userinfo.email, calendar.calendarlist.readonly, calendar.events, calendar.events.freebusy.
  • Drive: search existing files, read supported content, export documents, create text files and folders, and copy files where permitted. No original-file overwrite, arbitrary binary upload or structured Docs/Sheets/Slides editing. Returned files are limited to 8 MiB each. Scopes: openid, userinfo.email, drive.readonly, drive.file.

Disconnect, revoke and delete

  1. Stop use in Doo: open connector details, select Disconnect and confirm. Configuration and encrypted authorization data remain for reconnection, along with existing chats, recovery records and files.
  2. Revoke Google access: visit your Google Account's third-party connections and remove Doo's access. Other Google services using the same Doo app may also need reauthorization.
  3. Manage saved content: delete relevant chats or files in Doo. Removing a visible item does not certify removal of all internal task recovery copies. For complete cloud content and credential cleanup, use account deletion or contact support.
  4. Delete your Doo account: choose account deletion in the account page in Settings, then complete fresh Apple or email verification. Accepted requests stop account access while cloud content, tasks and credentials are cleaned up. Cleanup can remain pending after a failure; check the app's private deletion receipt. Necessary transaction and deletion receipts follow the privacy policy.
  5. External copies: Google drafts, events and files created by Doo are managed separately in Google. Manage your exports and independent device copies separately too.
