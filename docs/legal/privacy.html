1. 本机内容与账号

你可以在未登录时使用可用的本机功能。访客内容、本机账号内容及不同账号分别保存。登录不会自动将访客内容上传或合并到账号。普通退出登录会保留该账号的本机内容；在“账号”中选择清除本机数据会删除该账号在此设备上的内容、服务凭据、内容密钥和本机备份。请先导出需要的内容，并另行保存备份密钥。

数据保存位置与用途
Apple 登录身份、账号 UUID、账号状态、会话及协议同意记录Supabase Auth 和账号数据库，用于验证身份、控制访问、注销和必要的安全处理。Apple 可能提供真实或隐藏邮件地址；App 不要求通讯录或公开社交资料。
Doo 名字、外观颜色和性格选项少量账号资料保存在 Supabase，用于同账号设备的一致设置。
购买商品、会员状态、交易环境与最小处理回执Apple、RevenueCat 和 Doo 账号数据库处理购买、恢复、退款或到期后的权益核验。Doo 不处理你的银行卡号。
对话、作品、制作的文件和图片、资料原件、个人技能、可编辑记忆默认只保存在本机。六类同步开关分别默认关闭；仅在你开启对应范围并确认恢复密钥后，才将该范围的内容在设备端加密后上传至 Supabase。
模型/API/MCP 服务凭据、设备权限、检索索引、设备设置、Cookie、运行状态和执行任务留在本机；凭据使用 Keychain。它们不加入私人内容同步或用户内容备份。连接到你选择的服务时，会按该连接协议向相应服务发送必要凭据。

2. 加密同步与备份

私人内容同步使用设备端认证加密。服务器保存密文、加密后的内容密钥及处理同步所需的元数据，例如账号、对象标识、类别、修订、容量、时间和删除状态。加密不隐藏这些元数据。恢复密钥由你保存，Doo 和 Supabase 不持有可替你解密私人内容的恢复密钥。

新设备需要输入恢复密钥才能解密。若恢复密钥及所有已解锁设备都丢失，我们不能恢复该私人内容。更换恢复密钥只更新密钥包装，不自动撤销已经持有内容密钥的设备。请妥善保护设备；不需要保留的设备可执行本机数据清除。登录凭据被撤销后会停止云端访问，但远程撤销不会自动抹除其他设备已下载的内容。

你可以导出独立加密的 .dooarchive 文件。备份密钥与云同步恢复密钥不同，必须与文件分开保管。共享或保存到其他 App 后，副本由对应位置的访问与保留规则管理。备份恢复会校验完整性，保留现有数据；其他账号可将可复制的内容导入为新副本。权限需要重新授予，任务和外部动作不会重放。含有现有内容的单一资料项可能被保留而不导入旧版本。

配置启用后的服务运营备份会将账号数据库、最小 Apple 身份资料和已加密的 Storage 文件另行加密后存入 Cloudflare R2 私有存储。运营备份不包含登录会话或你的私人内容解密密钥；用于备份加密的运营密钥与云同步密钥不同。运营备份用于故障恢复，设置 30 天保留期；恢复前必须使用最新的删除记录，不能重新启用已删除账号或已清除的内容。

3. AI 与外部服务

“内容留本机”是存储安排，不表示 AI 推理离线。你发送对话、生成或修改作品、检索资料、整理记忆、调用 MCP 或插件时，选用的服务可能接收完成该请求所需的输入、正文、代码或附件。这个传输与可选的加密云同步是不同流程，相关服务会按自己的政策处理请求。请仅发送你有权提供的内容，并核对所选连接与权限。

Doo 的本机检索索引可由原件重建，不通过账号同步上传检索向量、OCR 缓存或中间提取文本。作品或外部内容不能取得宿主登录凭据、恢复密钥或自行增加设备权限。

4. 设备权限、诊断与服务位置

录音、照片、位置、提醒事项等系统能力只有在相关功能需要时按系统权限流程使用。拒绝权限时，相应功能可能不可用。语音转文字按设备实际支持的系统能力运行。作品权限按作品及账号隔离，不随导入或登录自动继承。

必要的技术处理可能涉及网络地址、请求时间、错误代码、设备或系统版本等信息。Doo 的普通诊断不记录私人正文、完整输入输出或密钥；用户主动导出诊断时应查看具体范围。Apple、Supabase、RevenueCat、Cloudflare 及你选择的服务也可能处理自己的服务日志。

官方 Supabase 项目选择新加坡区域。其他供应商可能在其服务地区处理必要数据；数据可能跨境传输。服务器地区不表示访问仅限该地区，也不保证所有网络条件下连接均稳定。请查阅 Supabase、RevenueCat、Cloudflare 和 Apple 的隐私政策。

5. 你的选择与删除

  • 你可以关闭各类同步。关闭停止后续同步，已有云端内容仍保留；需要删除已有云端内容时使用“清除云端内容”。该操作保留本机原件与账号，关闭六类同步并重置同步密钥。
  • 你可以在本机编辑、删除内容，导出加密备份，或清除此账号的本机数据。清除本机数据不删除云端内容或其他账号的内容。
  • 注销需要重新验证同一 Apple 身份。请求被接受后立即停止该账号的云端访问，随后撤销 Apple 授权、清理 RevenueCat 关联、云端内容及 Auth 账号。App 会保存私有状态回执，尚未完成的清理会重试；完成状态确认后清理此设备上该账号的数据。
  • 账号删除不会自动取消 App Store 订阅。请在 Apple 的订阅管理中另行处理订阅、退款或账单。
  • 已经离线下载的其他设备副本及你另行分享的备份不能由服务器远程保证删除。请在相应设备和保存位置清除。

当前在线服务内容在清理过程中删除；运营备份中的密文按 30 天保留规则到期。防止故障恢复后重新启用已删除内容所必需的最小删除记录会继续保留，它们不包含私人正文或解密密钥。依法或为商店交易处理必须保留的记录，以及供应商独立保留的交易或安全记录，按对应要求处理。联系邮箱可用于提出访问、更正、删除或其他隐私请求。

6. 更新与联系

如数据处理范围、供应商或用途发生实质变化，我们会更新政策并在需要时重新取得同意。遇到恢复密钥丢失时，我们只能协助核对账号与功能，不能绕过私人内容加密。

Doo Privacy Policy

Updated October 11, 2026. Provider: WENLU ZHANG (张文禄). Privacy contact: jinwandalaohu940@gmail.com.

Local content and essential account data

Doo is an iOS app for conversations, creating files, and building and running personal interactive works. Guest storage and each signed-in account have separate local storage. Signing in does not upload or merge guest content. Ordinary sign-out retains local content; clearing an account from this device removes its local content, credentials, content keys and saved backups. Export what you need and save its key separately first.

Supabase handles the Apple identity, stable account UUID, session, account status and consent records. Apple may supply a real or relay email address. Name, appearance and personality are minimal account settings. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases; Doo stores a minimal verified entitlement projection and processing receipts. Doo does not process your payment card number.

Optional encrypted sync and backups

Conversations, works, created files and images, library originals, personal skills and editable memories stay local by default. All six sync options start off. Only explicitly enabled content is encrypted on the device and uploaded after you confirm a recovery key. Servers can see required metadata, including account and object identifiers, category, revision, size, time and deletion state; encryption does not hide that metadata. Doo and Supabase do not hold the recovery key needed to decrypt private content.

A new device needs your recovery key. Losing the key and every unlocked device makes that content unrecoverable. Changing the recovery key rewraps the content key and does not revoke devices that already hold it. Revoking sign-in stops cloud access but does not remotely erase downloaded copies.

A .dooarchive export has its own independent encryption and backup key. Keep the key separate from the file. Exports saved or shared elsewhere are governed by that location. Import verifies integrity, retains existing data and can create new copies for another account. Permissions are not imported and tasks or external actions do not run. An item with existing content may be retained instead of replaced by an older version.

When configured, operational backups independently encrypt the account database, minimal Apple identity records and encrypted Storage files into a private Cloudflare R2 bucket. They exclude sign-in sessions and users’ content decryption keys. Operational encryption keys are separate from user keys. Backups have a 30-day retention policy. Recovery must use the latest deletion ledger and must not reactivate erased accounts or cleared content.

AI, credentials and device access

Local storage does not imply offline AI inference. Conversations, generation, edits, library queries, memory organization, MCP and plugin actions may send required text, code or attachments to the service you selected. This is separate from optional encrypted sync. Review the selected service, its policy and the permissions before sending content.

Model/API/MCP credentials use local Keychain storage and are sent only as required to the selected connection. Credentials, permissions, device preferences, cookies, indexes and execution tasks are excluded from private sync and user backups. Local indexes can be rebuilt from originals. This account system does not use Mem0. Generated or imported content cannot grant itself host permissions or access account and recovery credentials.

System access such as microphone, photos, location and reminders is requested when the relevant feature needs it. Permissions remain scoped and are not automatically inherited on import. Speech recognition follows the capabilities actually available on the device. Necessary technical handling may include network addresses, request times, error codes and device/system versions. Ordinary Doo diagnostics exclude private bodies, complete inputs/outputs and secrets; review the scope of a diagnostic export you choose to share. Providers may maintain their own service logs.

Providers, location and your choices

The official Supabase project uses Singapore. Other providers may process necessary data in their service regions, including internationally. Region selection does not restrict access to that region or guarantee connectivity. Provider policies are linked in the Chinese section above.

You may disable any sync category; that stops subsequent sync but does not remove existing cloud content. “Clear cloud content” retains local originals and the account, disables all six categories and resets sync keys. Local cleanup leaves cloud content and other accounts intact.

Account deletion requires fresh verification of the same Apple identity. Acceptance immediately blocks cloud access. Background cleanup revokes Apple authorization, removes the RevenueCat association, cloud content and Auth account, and retries unfinished work. A private receipt lets the app check status; confirmed completion clears that account’s data on this device. Deleting the account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. Manage subscriptions, billing and refunds through Apple. Other offline devices and separately shared exports require separate cleanup.

Online data is removed during cleanup; operational backup ciphertext expires under the 30-day policy. Minimal deletion records needed to prevent restoration of erased content remain, without private bodies or decryption keys. Records required for lawful obligations or store transactions, and providers’ independent transaction/security records, follow applicable requirements. Contact us to request access, correction, deletion or other privacy assistance. Material changes to processing will update this policy and require new consent where needed. We cannot bypass lost content encryption keys.
