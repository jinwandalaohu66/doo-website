1. 本机资料与轻账号

对话、作品、文件、图片、资料原件、个人技能、名字、外观、性格及可编辑记忆保存在本机，不通过账号上传。登录、退出和注销不切换或删除本机资料库。共享设备上的其他使用者可能看到设备上已有资料，请使用设备锁屏等保护措施。旧版本的本机资料在升级时保留，并在本机整理为同一资料库；冲突原件保留在本机恢复材料中。

数据保存位置与用途
账号 UUID、Apple 身份的不可直接读取索引、账号状态、协议版本和同意时间阿里云华东 1（杭州）的 Doo 专属数据库，用于验证身份、稳定购买关联及注销。登录不要求姓名、邮箱或通讯录。
账号会话和 Apple 授权撤销所需凭据设备会话保存在 Keychain；服务端保存会话校验信息及加密的 Apple 撤销凭据，用于访问控制、退出及注销。
购买关联、核验后的会员状态、商品与交易处理回执Apple 和 RevenueCat 处理购买、恢复、退款与续订；Doo 后台保存必要的权益投影和去重回执。Doo 不处理银行卡号。
额度账户、周期、余额、预留和结算记录Doo 专属数据库，用于在相关付费服务启用后核验余额和防止重复扣费。账号账本不保存聊天正文、作品或完整模型回复；价格与开放状态以实际界面为准。
模型、图片及 MCP 服务凭据，Cookie、设备权限、索引、运行状态与任务留在本机，秘密使用 Keychain。连接你选用的服务时，按该服务的协议发送必要凭据；不通过账号上传。

2. AI 和外部服务

本机存储不代表模型推理离线。发送对话、生成或修改作品、检索资料、整理记忆、使用 MCP 或插件时，你选用的模型及外部服务会接收完成请求所需的输入、上下文、代码或附件，并按各自政策处理。请核对连接地址、服务条款和授予的权限。账号服务与这些传输分开，不因登录而取得私人内容。生成内容不能取得宿主账号凭据或自行增加设备权限。

3. 本机加密备份

你可导出独立加密的 .dooarchive 文件。备份密钥保存在此设备的受保护存储，查看时需要系统验证；请另行将密钥与备份文件分开保管。密钥和所有可解锁设备丢失时，我们无法替你解密。导出到其他 App 或存储位置的副本由该位置管理。本机备份不是自动云备份；备份不包含账号会话、模型或 MCP 秘密。导入校验完整性并保留现有成果，不重放任务、外部动作或设备授权。

4. 注销、保留和迁移

你可在账号页重新验证 Apple 身份后发起注销。请求获接受后停止账号访问，后台撤销 Apple 授权并清理身份凭据、会话和账号权益投影。网络失败时 App 保留私有查询回执，不将未知状态显示为完成。本机内容、服务配置及已导出的文件保留，由你自行管理。注销不会自动取消 App Store 订阅，请在 Apple 订阅管理中另行取消。

为完成退款、交易去重及避免恢复已注销身份，可能保留必要的交易和删除记录；它们不包含私人内容或有效登录秘密。供应商依法或为交易安全独立保存的记录遵循其政策。旧 Supabase 系统仅在受控迁移期间用于核对原身份和会员关联，原有私人同步停止；迁移核验后清理旧服务及运营备份，不将其内容导入新的账号服务。升级后的本机资料不依赖旧云服务。

5. 权限、诊断和服务地区

录音、照片、位置及其他系统能力在使用时请求权限，导入资料不继承设备授权。普通诊断只包含必要的技术信息，如请求时间、状态、错误码和系统版本，不记录完整对话、回复或秘密。服务商可能维护其自身的访问日志。请检查主动分享的诊断范围。

账号服务位于中国大陆杭州；Apple、RevenueCat 和你选用的模型或外部服务可能在境外处理必要信息。联网能力取决于实际网络和供应商，不保证所有网络始终可达。参阅 阿里云隐私政策、Apple 隐私政策及 RevenueCat 隐私政策。你可以通过联系邮箱提出访问、更正、删除或其他隐私请求。处理范围有实质变化时更新本政策，并在需要时重新取得同意。

Doo Privacy Policy

Updated October 12, 2026. Provider: WENLU ZHANG (张文禄). Contact: jinwandalaohu940@gmail.com.

Local content and accounts

Conversations, works, files, images, library originals, personal skills, appearance, personality and editable memories stay on your device. Sign-in, sign-out and account deletion do not switch or erase that library. Other users of a shared device may see its existing content; protect your device. Upgrades retain previous local content and organize it into one local library, preserving conflicting originals in local recovery materials.

Alibaba Cloud in Hangzhou, mainland China, hosts the account UUID, a non-readable Apple identity index, account status and consent records. Sign-in does not request your name, email or contacts. Device sessions use Keychain. The backend stores session verification information and encrypted Apple credentials needed to revoke authorization. Apple and RevenueCat handle payments; Doo retains necessary verified entitlement projections and transaction receipts, not payment card numbers.

The credit ledger holds balances, periods, reservations and settlement records when the relevant paid services are enabled. It excludes chat bodies, works and complete model outputs. Availability and pricing follow the actual app interface. Model, image and MCP credentials, cookies, permissions, indexes and tasks remain local. Credentials are sent only as required to the services you select.

AI, backups and deletion

Local storage does not mean offline inference. Selected AI and external services receive inputs, context, code or attachments needed for requests and process them under their policies. Account services do not receive private content merely because you sign in. Generated content cannot obtain account secrets or grant itself device permissions.

You may export an encrypted .dooarchive. Its key is protected on this device and viewing it requires system authentication. Keep the key separately. We cannot decrypt a backup after all keys and unlocked devices are lost. Exports are managed by their destination. This is not automatic cloud backup. Archives exclude account sessions and model/MCP secrets. Import checks integrity, retains existing work and does not replay tasks, actions or permissions.

Deletion requires fresh Apple verification. Acceptance stops account access; cleanup revokes Apple authorization and removes identity credentials, sessions and account entitlement projections. A private receipt supports status checks after network failure. Local content, service configurations and exports remain under your control. Deletion does not cancel App Store subscriptions; manage them through Apple.

Necessary transaction and deletion records may remain for refunds, deduplication and preventing restoration of erased identities; they contain neither private content nor usable login secrets. Providers may retain records independently. During controlled migration, the former Supabase service is used only to verify original identities and purchase associations. Private sync stops, and old services and operational backups are removed after verification. The new account service does not import that private content.

Permissions, location and requests

System permissions are requested when needed. Imported content does not inherit grants. Ordinary diagnostics exclude complete chats, outputs and secrets and include only necessary technical information. Review diagnostic exports you choose to share. Providers may keep their own access logs.

Account services run in Hangzhou. Apple, RevenueCat and selected AI/external services may process necessary data internationally. Connectivity depends on networks and providers.
